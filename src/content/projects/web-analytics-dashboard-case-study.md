I created a **dashboard** to bring website traffic, SEO visibility, paid campaigns, and lead tracking into one reporting environment.

The goal was to help the client explore digital performance through clear summaries and detailed views, with filters for location, device, campaign, and website section. All screenshots have been anonymized to protect client confidentiality.

## Project Outcome

The project delivered a reporting environment that connects acquisition, website usage, campaign performance, and contact actions.

Its value lies in the ability to move from a high-level overview to focused analysis by channel, content, device, territory, and customer segment—giving the client a clearer structure for reviewing digital performance.
